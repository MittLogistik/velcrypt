KEEP A DECOY (PREMIUM)

YOUR PATTERN, NOT YOUR FACE

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

• Albums, search, grid or list view

ENCRYPTED BACKUP
Back up a vault to one encrypted file, protected by a password you choose. Keep it in iCloud Drive or on a computer, and restore it on a new iPhone.
