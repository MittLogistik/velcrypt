## Description (1,478/4,000)

```
Velcrypt is a private vault for the photos, videos and documents you don't want anyone else to see. Everything is encrypted on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM. No account. No cloud.

ONE PATTERN, ONE VAULT
You unlock Velcrypt by drawing a pattern on a 5×5 grid of dots. Every pattern opens its own separate vault, and Velcrypt never shows a list of vaults. A pattern it hasn't seen before simply opens an empty vault: no error, no hint that anything else exists.

KEEP A DECOY
Fill a second vault with harmless photos. If you're ever pressured to open the app, draw that pattern instead. A duress pattern is coming soon.

ENCRYPTED ON YOUR IPHONE
• AES-256-GCM encryption for every photo, video and document
• Your pattern is turned into the key on your device. Nobody else has a copy, not even us.
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud
• Vault data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups

YOUR PATTERN, NOT YOUR FACE
Velcrypt deliberately has no PIN and no Face ID. The key lives in your head.

ORGANISE
• Import from Photos or Files
• Albums inside each vault
• Search by name and album
• Grid or list view, light and dark themes
• Auto-Lock

PLEASE NOTE
• A forgotten pattern cannot be recovered. Without it, that vault can never be opened again.
• Importing copies your items. The originals stay in Photos or Files until you delete them there.
• Vaults aren't backed up, so if you lose or reset your iPhone, the vaults are lost too.

Velcrypt. Well Encrypted. Truly Private.
```
