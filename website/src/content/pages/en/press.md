## Boilerplate

### 50 words

Velcrypt is an iPhone app for private photos, videos and documents. Files are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM and opened by drawing a pattern. Every pattern is its own vault, and unknown patterns open an empty one. There is no account, no cloud and no ads.

### 100 words

Velcrypt is a native iPhone app that keeps private photos, videos and documents in encrypted vaults on the device. Instead of Face ID or a PIN, you open a vault by drawing a pattern on a 5×5 grid of dots — a secret that lives only in your memory. Every pattern is its own vault, and a pattern the app has never seen opens an empty one, so nothing reveals how many vaults exist. Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM through Apple CryptoKit. There is no account, no server and no tracking of vault content. Velcrypt has a free version and an optional Premium upgrade.

## Writing about Velcrypt

A few points help keep coverage accurate:

- Velcrypt has **not** been independently audited. Please don’t describe it as audited.
- Encrypted backup is available and free. The duress pattern is planned, not available.
- Velcrypt can’t recover forgotten patterns; that’s a deliberate consequence of having no account or server.
- There is no press coverage to cite yet, and we don’t publish user numbers or ratings.
