Most questions about Velcrypt are answered in the [FAQ](id:faq). If yours isn’t, or something isn’t working as expected, we’re happy to help by email.

## Contact us

Email **support@velcrypt.com**. For privacy questions, write to privacy@velcrypt.com; for media enquiries, see the [press kit](id:press).

There is no account and no in-app chat, so email is the only channel. We only receive what you choose to send.

## What to include

To help us understand the problem quickly, please include:

1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
2. The Velcrypt version, shown in Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Velcrypt.
3. What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
4. The steps that lead to the problem, if you can reproduce it.
5. Any error message, written out in words.

Please **don’t** send your pattern, and don’t attach screenshots that show private content. We never need either.

## What support cannot do

Velcrypt is built so that nobody but you can open your vaults — including us. That means support **cannot**:

- recover or reset a forgotten pattern,
- restore a vault from a lost, broken or reset iPhone,
- tell you how many vaults exist on a device,
- decrypt files or retrieve content for you.

This is not a policy we could change on request; it’s how the encryption works. The [security model](id:security) explains why, and [forgot your vault pattern](id:forgot-vault-pattern) covers what you can still try yourself.

## Quick troubleshooting

### My pattern opens an empty vault

This is the most common question. Velcrypt has no “wrong pattern” message: any pattern it hasn’t seen before simply opens a new, empty vault. If you expected your photos, you most likely drew a slightly different pattern — an extra dot, a missed corner, or a different starting point.

Lock the app, then draw your pattern again slowly and deliberately. Try any close variations you remember. Your original vault is still there, untouched; the empty one doesn’t replace it.

### The app locks too fast

Velcrypt locks when it goes to the background, after a delay you can set. If it locks while you briefly switch to another app, increase the auto-lock delay in Velcrypt’s settings. A shorter delay is safer, so choose the shortest one that still works for you. See [auto-lock](id:auto-lock).

### Import fails or the app runs out of space

Encrypted copies take roughly as much space as the originals. Check free storage in Settings › General › iPhone Storage. Remember that importing doesn’t remove originals from Photos; deleting them, and emptying Recently Deleted, frees space — see [deleting originals after import](id:delete-original-photos-after-import).

### I can’t find a file I imported

Check other albums within the same vault, and make sure you opened the vault you think you did. Items in one vault are never visible from another.
