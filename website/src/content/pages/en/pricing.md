Velcrypt is designed so that the core promise — your private files encrypted on your own iPhone — is the same for everyone. Paying adds room and convenience, not a better lock.

## Free and Premium

Velcrypt is a free download with an optional Premium upgrade.

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Encrypted vaults with pattern unlock | Yes | Yes |
| AES-256-GCM encryption, on-device only | Yes | Yes |
| Albums, viewer, in-vault camera, secure notes, document import, secure export | Yes | Yes |
| Auto-lock and app-switcher privacy | Yes | Yes |
| Encrypted backup | Yes | Yes |
| Number of items | Up to 50 | Unlimited |
| Number of vaults | 1 | More vaults (each pattern opens its own) |
| Duress pattern | — | Yes |
| Alternate app icons | — | Yes |
| Ads | None | None |

Because the free version has one vault, a second vault — such as a [decoy vault](id:hidden-vaults) — needs Premium. So does the duress pattern, which you set up from inside a decoy vault.

## Premium prices

| Plan | Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| Weekly | $1.99 per week |
| Yearly | $19.99 per year, with a 3-day free trial |
| Lifetime | $39.99, one-time purchase |

App Store prices vary by country. The App Store always shows the price in your currency before you confirm a purchase.

**About subscriptions.** Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The weekly and yearly plans renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. The yearly free trial converts to a paid yearly subscription unless you cancel before it ends. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in your Apple Account settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on another iPhone with **Restore Purchases**.

## What every version includes

Security is not a paid tier. Every vault, free or Premium, uses the same design described on the [security model](id:security) page: keys derived from your pattern with PBKDF2, a random master key per vault, and AES-256-GCM encryption through Apple CryptoKit.

There is also no account in any version. You don’t sign up, you don’t give an email address, and nothing you store leaves your iPhone unless you export it or save a backup file yourself.

## Why Premium, not ads

A privacy app that earns money from advertising or data has a conflict of interest built into it. Velcrypt avoids that by asking the people who want more from the app to pay for it directly. Everyone else keeps a fully encrypted free version with no strings attached.

That model also shapes what goes into Premium. Paid features add capacity — more items, more vaults — or extras like the [duress pattern](id:duress-pattern) and alternate app icons. They never unlock a “real” level of encryption that the free version lacks, and encrypted backup stays free so nobody has to pay to protect their files against a lost phone. A duress pattern you have already set up keeps working even if Premium lapses; only setting one up needs Premium.

If you upgrade later, nothing about your existing vault changes. Your data stays on your iPhone, encrypted the same way, whether or not you upgrade.

## Our promise

- **No ads.** Velcrypt does not show advertising, in the free version or in Premium.
- **No selling data.** Velcrypt doesn’t collect your vault content or analytics about it, so there is nothing to sell. See the [privacy policy](id:privacy).
