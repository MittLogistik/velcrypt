Keepsafe is one of the best-known photo vault apps, and plenty of people are happy with it. Others start looking around after a while, usually because of how it’s built rather than anything dramatic. This page covers the common reasons, what to look for in any replacement, the realistic options on iPhone and how to move your photos without leaving copies behind.

## Why people look for an alternative

None of these are flaws in themselves; they’re design choices that suit some people and not others.

- **The account.** According to its App Store listing, Keepsafe uses an account linked to an email address. Some people would rather a private vault didn’t know who they are.
- **The cloud.** Keepsafe offers an optional cloud backup with its premium plan. That’s a benefit if you want recoverability, and a concern if you want no copy off the device.
- **The subscription.** Some features sit behind a recurring subscription. Whether that’s worth it is personal.
- **Platform or habits change.** A new phone, a new threat, or simply wanting fewer features.

## What to look for in any photo vault

A photo vault is an app that stores selected photos and videos separately from your main library behind its own lock. Before you switch, check these points in any candidate. The guide [are photo vault apps safe?](id:are-photo-vault-apps-safe) goes further.

1. **Is encryption documented?** Look for named algorithms and how keys are derived, not just “secure”.
2. **Is an account required?** If not, how is your data recovered, if at all?
3. **Where are copies kept?** On the device only, in the developer’s cloud, in iCloud backups?
4. **How does it unlock?** PIN, pattern, passcode, biometrics, and whether a secret is separate from your device passcode.
5. **What happens if you forget the code?** Some apps offer recovery; some can’t, by design.
6. **Does it support what you store?** Photos, videos, documents.

**Velcrypt** keeps everything on the iPhone, with no account and no servers. You unlock by drawing a pattern on a 5×5 grid, and every pattern opens its own vault; an unknown pattern opens an empty one, which gives you [plausible deniability](id:plausible-deniability-explained). Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The honest trade-off: vault data isn’t included in iCloud or computer backups and a forgotten pattern can’t be undone; to survive a lost phone, you create an encrypted backup file yourself in Settings → Backup. The [direct comparison with Keepsafe](id:velcrypt-vs-keepsafe) sets the two side by side.

**Apple’s built-ins** cost nothing. The [Hidden album](id:velcrypt-vs-iphone-hidden-album) is easy and syncs, but opens with your device passcode. [Locked notes](id:velcrypt-vs-apple-notes-locked-notes) suit a few sensitive images or scans.

**Other vault apps** vary widely in encryption, accounts and cloud features. The roundup of [private photo vault apps](id:best-private-photo-vault-apps-iphone) compares several.

## When staying with Keepsafe may make sense

If cloud backup is the feature you rely on most, and you’re comfortable with an account, Keepsafe may still be the better fit. Velcrypt doesn’t offer automatic cloud backup or sync — only a manual encrypted backup file — and it isn’t available on Android.

## How to switch without leaving copies

The safest switch is boring and methodical. These steps are generic; your old app’s menus may differ.

1. **Export from the old app.** Use Keepsafe’s export or save option to move items back to the Photos app or to Files. Check its help pages for the current steps.
2. **Check the export.** Make sure everything arrived, including videos, before deleting anything.
3. **Import into the new vault.** In Velcrypt, open the vault for your pattern and import from Photos or Files. Importing copies the items.
4. **Delete the exported copies.** Remove them from Photos, then empty Recently Deleted; otherwise they stay there for about 30 days. See [delete original photos after import](id:delete-original-photos-after-import).
5. **Clean up the old app.** Delete items inside it, delete any cloud data and account through its settings or support, then remove the app.
